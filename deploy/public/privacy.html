Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED  ·  JUNE 3, 2026

Verity One ("Verity One," "we," "us," or "our") provides a personal, reviewable knowledge-graph and vault-sync service. This Privacy Policy explains what information we access, how we use it, and the choices you have — with particular attention to information accessed through Google APIs. Your local node is the source of truth; we broker, we do not own.

1. Information we access

  • Google account profile. When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, basic profile information (such as your name), and a Google account identifier. We use this solely to authenticate you and to associate your session with your Verity One account.
  • Google Drive — narrow per-file access (drive.file). If you choose to connect Google Drive, Verity One requests only the per-file https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This grants access only to files and folders that Verity One itself creates, or that you explicitly open with Verity One — it does not grant access to your entire Google Drive. We use it to create a dedicated "Verity One" folder in your Drive and to synchronize the notes and documents in your Verity One knowledge vault to and from that folder.

2. How we use Google user data

We use the access described above exclusively to provide the features you enable:

  • Sign-in: your Google profile authenticates you to Verity One.
  • Vault sync: the drive.file scope is used only to mirror your Verity One vault between your own device(s) and your own Google Drive. We do not read, index, or process any Drive files outside the Verity One folder.

Your vault content remains in your Google Drive and on your local Verity One node. Verity One brokers the synchronization; it is not a service that retains a separate copy of your vault.

3. How your Google credentials are handled

The OAuth authorization is brokered by our hosted service (verityone.app) and delivered to your local Verity One node over an end-to-end encrypted ("sealed") channel; the resulting access token is held by your local node. We never receive or store your Google password. The credential hand-off is single-use and short-lived, and credentials are encrypted in transit and at rest during that hand-off.

4. Limited Use disclosure

Verity One's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically: we use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features that are prominent in Verity One; we do not sell this data; we do not use it for advertising; we do not transfer it to others except as necessary to provide or improve the feature, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition; and humans do not read your Google user data except with your consent for specific files, where necessary for security purposes (such as investigating abuse), or to comply with applicable law.

5. Data we do not access

We do not access your full Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, or any other Google data beyond the scopes listed in Section 1. We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can revoke Verity One's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by disconnecting Google Drive from your Verity One dashboard.
  • Revoking access stops further synchronization. Files already synced to your Drive remain under your control in your own Google Drive.
  • You may request deletion of your Verity One account data by contacting us at the address below.

7. Data retention

We retain account and authorization metadata only for as long as your Verity One account is active or as needed to provide the service. When you disconnect Drive or delete your account, associated authorization tokens are revoked and removed.

8. Children

Verity One is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@verityone.app.
